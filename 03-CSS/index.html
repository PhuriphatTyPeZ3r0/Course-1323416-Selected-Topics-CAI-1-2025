Gojo Satoru

Gojo Satoru (五条 悟, Gojō Satoru) is a fictional character from the manga and anime series Jujutsu Kaisen created by Gege Akutami. He is one of the main characters in the series and serves as a powerful jujutsu sorcerer and a teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High School. Gojo is known for his exceptional strength, speed, and combat skills, making him one of the most formidable sorcerers in the Jujutsu Kaisen universe. He possesses the Limitless Cursed Technique, which allows him to manipulate space and create an infinite void around him, making it nearly impossible for opponents to land a hit on him.

Itadori Yuji

Itadori Yuji (虎杖 悠仁, Itadori Yūji) is the main protagonist of the manga and anime series Jujutsu Kaisen created by Gege Akutami. He is a high school student who becomes involved in the world of jujutsu sorcery after ingesting a powerful cursed object, a finger belonging to the legendary curse Sukuna Ryoumen. Yuuji is known for his exceptional physical abilities, including superhuman strength, speed, and agility. Despite his initial reluctance to get involved in the dangerous world of jujutsu sorcery, he quickly adapts and becomes a skilled fighter under the guidance of his mentor, Gojo Satoru.

Okkotsu Yuta

Okkotsu Yuta (乙骨 憂太, Okkotsu Yūta) is a fictional character from the manga and anime series Jujutsu Kaisen created by Gege Akutami. He is a special grade jujutsu sorcerer and one of the main characters in the series. Yuta is known for his exceptional cursed energy and combat skills, making him one of the most powerful sorcerers in the Jujutsu Kaisen universe. He possesses a unique ability called "Cursed Speech," which allows him to manipulate and control others through his words. This ability makes him a formidable opponent in battles against curses and other sorcerers.
